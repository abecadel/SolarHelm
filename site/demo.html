PLANNER DEMO

A one-minute tour of the companion app. Every number in the video is computed by the app's real code — the render script feeds the same modules the planner runs in your browser.

What you're seeing

1 · Describe the trip
Position, distance, days, cruise strategy, battery limits. One form.
2 · Hour-by-hour plan
PV, motor power and SOC simulated against the forecast with the firmware's reserve philosophy.
3 · The verdict
Trip fits or it doesn't, arrival time and SOC, energy split — day-level accounting.
4 · Voyage A→B
Waypoints in; the route optimizer sweeps departure times and power settings; six named safety gates explain the SAFE/POSSIBLE verdict.
5 · It learns
A telemetry log refits the hull curve, calibrates uncertainty and watches for drift. Every voyage improves the next prediction.
Advisory only
The phone plans; the boat executes and protects. The link can die at any moment and nothing unsafe happens.

The demo is rendered with Remotion from the demo/ project in the repository, which imports the planner's actual modules for its data. No screenshots were faked. Prefer the real thing? The planner runs offline in any browser: open the app.
