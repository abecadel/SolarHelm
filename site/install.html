Read docs/SAFETY.md first. SolarHelm only generates a low-power throttle signal. It never switches propulsion current, the kill switch never passes through it, and MANUAL is the hardware default whenever the controller is unpowered.

High-current path

  PV array ──► MPPT ─────────────┐
                                 │
 (+) Battery ── main fuse ── battery switch ──┬── MPPT battery (+)
      │        (at the post,                  ├── contactor ── motor controller ── motor
      │         before anything)              └── DC/DC 24→12 V ── hotel loads
 (−) Battery ── SmartShunt ──┬────────────────┴── common negative bus
                             └── EVERY load's return goes through
                                 the shunt, or SOC lies

Four rules, no exceptions:

Fuse first
Main fuse directly at the battery positive post, sized above continuous draw and below the cable's ampacity. Calculate, don't guess.
Shunt sees everything
SmartShunt in the negative lead, battery side of all loads and chargers. One bypassed return corrupts the control input.
Kill switch is independent
The lanyard switch drives the motor contactor directly. SolarHelm only senses it.
Cold PV voltage
Panel series strings must stay under the MPPT's Voc limit at low temperature — Voc rises when cold; check the datasheet coefficient.

Control wiring

 24 V bus ─► isolated DC/DC ─► ESP32-S3
 SmartShunt VE.Direct TX ────► UART (3.3 V — never level-shift to 5 V)
 GNSS ───────────────────────► UART
 GP8403 DAC ◄──── I2C ────────┤
 MANUAL/AUTO switch ─────────► GPIO (pull-up, switch to GND)
 heartbeat GPIO ─► monostable ─► AUTO relay coil

 GP8403 OUT0 ─► hardware clamp ─► relay NO ─┐
 Manual throttle pot ──────────── relay NC ─┼─► motor controller
 relay common ──────────────────────────────┘   throttle input

The relay is normally closed on the manual pot: with SolarHelm dead, unpowered, or crashed, the helm works as if it were never installed. The relay coil is held by a toggling heartbeat through a monostable — a hung microcontroller stops the edges and the boat drops to MANUAL in hardware. Pin numbers live in firmware/pins.h; ground the control side at exactly one point (the isolated DC/DC makes this possible).

Commissioning: three gated phases

A — Bench (no propulsion)
Scope the DAC's cold-boot 0 V. Sweep the throttle chain into a meter. Live VE.Direct and GNSS. Prove the relay drops on a firmware hang. Watchdog and brownout tests. End-to-end HIL with replayed data.
B — Motor bench
Open the motor head and confirm its topology before buying the controller. Calibrate the controller with the prop off. Add contactor, kill switch and shunt in the real harness. AUTO engagement, ramp and fault tests. Load soak with envelope caps verified.
C — On the water
Dock trials, MANUAL baseline, SOLAR-mode maiden with two crew, power-ladder efficiency runs that produce your boat's real curve, then solar commissioning and a phone-link voyage rehearsal.

Every gate has written acceptance criteria — do the steps, meet every criterion, log the results, and never proceed on a failed gate. Full protocol: docs/HARDWARE_TEST_PLAN.md.

Standing rules for every powered test

Lanyard kill switch worn from the first propulsion test on. Battery main switch within reach. Fuse before experiment. One change at a time. The motor never turns near people or lines; the prop never runs in air at speed. Log everything — the test log becomes the adaptive model's first dataset.
