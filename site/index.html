SOLARHELM

Energy-aware cruise control for solar-electric boats. Instead of holding a fixed speed, SolarHelm holds a fixed battery power — so your boat cruises on exactly the energy the sun provides.

The idea

A conventional cruise control holds speed and lets the battery pick up the difference — until it is empty. SolarHelm inverts this: it targets 0 W battery power and continuously trims the motor so propulsion follows solar production. Clouds pass, the boat gently slows; the sun returns, it speeds up. The battery stays full for when you actually need it. That is the whole trick — and it is enough to cruise all day, day after day, without shore power.

What it does

SOLAR mode
Target battery power 0 W. Cruise on sunshine alone; speed floats with the clouds.
SOLAR+ mode
Allow a configured battery contribution (e.g. −200 W) for a bit more speed.
Reserve floor
At your reserve SOC the controller refuses to consume further battery energy. Latched, with hysteresis, tested.
Fail-safe by design
Losing the shunt, the GPS or the microcontroller always means: automatic throttle to zero, manual helm has authority. The kill switch never passes through SolarHelm.
Learns your hull
Logs real Wh/km at every speed and builds your boat's efficiency curve from sea trials.
Plans your trip
The companion app fetches the solar/wind forecast and predicts range, SOC and arrival energy for the next days.

Simulation-first engineering

The identical C++ control core runs on the desktop simulator, in the unit tests (100% line coverage, enforced in CI) and on the ESP32-S3. Before any hardware existed, the controller already proved — with committed graphs — that it holds battery power at ≈0 W through 300–1500 W solar swings, ramps down safely when the sun vanishes, rises without overshoot, and never violates the SOC reserve.

Reference prototype (24 V, ~1.2 kW)

Drive
Storm N86 trolling motor + Kelly KDS24100E controller (0–5 V throttle input).
Brain
ESP32-S3 + GP8403 DAC behind a hardware MANUAL/AUTO relay and throttle clamp.
Senses
Victron SmartShunt (VE.Direct), u-blox GNSS, MPPT telemetry over RS485/VE.Direct.

SolarHelm is not coupled to these brands — drivers are plugins; this is simply the cheapest well-documented path to a working boat. Three costed BOMs in PLN: see the buying guide; wiring and commissioning: installation guide.

Safety first. SolarHelm generates a low-power throttle signal only; it never switches propulsion current. A normally-de-energized relay makes MANUAL the default whenever the controller is unpowered, and an independent kill switch always remains. Read docs/SAFETY.md before wiring anything.
