How to buy

Buy in three waves, gated by tests — never all at once. Wave 1 is cheap electronics you validate on a desk. Wave 2 (motor, controller, battery) only after the bench works and after opening the motor head to confirm its electrical topology. Wave 3 (solar) only after sea trials give you a real efficiency curve. The test protocol: docs/HARDWARE_TEST_PLAN.md.

Where cheap is fine — and where it isn't

Cheap is fine
ESP32 boards (clones work), GNSS modules, relays, enclosures, standard rigid residential solar panels — per watt they beat "marine" panels 6× with the same cells.
Don't cheap out
The shunt (it is the control input), the fuse/contactor/kill-switch chain, battery BMS quality, cable cross-sections and lugs.
Avoid
Flexible panels except on curved or walked surfaces (short life, hot spots), no-name LiFePO4 without documented BMS limits, un-fused anything.
